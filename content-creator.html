A simple way to start

I am testing a small prototype called Be Prepared. It helps you make a simple 5Q map for important life information: 1. What exists? 2. Where is it? 3. Who handles it? 4. Who else knows? 5. When was it last checked? It is not a password manager, not a document vault, and not a medical record. It stores directions, not secrets. Try it here: https://vid567.github.io/be-prepared-browser-beta/

Problem postConversation starter

The real question

A simple question: If someone you trust had to help you tomorrow, would they know where to find the important things? Not the secrets. Not the passwords. Not the private contents. Just the directions: what exists, where it is, who handles it, who else knows, and when it was last checked. That is the idea behind Be Prepared: https://vid567.github.io/be-prepared-browser-beta/

Privacy postTrust angle

Directions, not secrets

Be Prepared is built around one important rule: Directions, not secrets. Do not enter passwords, account numbers, medical details, or documents. The goal is only to help someone find the right information or the right person when they need to step in. Everything in this prototype stays in your browser. You can export a backup or print a PDF when you are ready. https://vid567.github.io/be-prepared-browser-beta/
